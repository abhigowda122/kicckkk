Coffee Liqueur

Kahlúa Coffee Liqueur

700 ml₹3,200/-

Kahlúa is a world-renowned coffee liqueur that brings the rich flavours of Mexican coffee to your glass. Made from 100% Arabica coffee beans, sugarcane rum, and a touch of vanilla, Kahlúa is known for its deep, roasted coffee aroma and a sweet, velvety smooth taste. It is a versatile spirit and a staple in many classic cocktails, from the timeless White Russian and Espresso Martini to a simple Kahlúa and milk.

Classic cocktails

White Russian

A creamy and iconic cocktail. Mix Kahlúa, vodka, and heavy cream or milk over ice.

Black Russian

A simpler version of the White Russian — just Kahlúa and vodka over ice.

Espresso Martini

A modern classic for coffee lovers. Shake Kahlúa, vodka and a shot of fresh espresso with ice for a frothy, caffeinated drink.

Mudslide

A decadent dessert in a glass — Kahlúa, vodka and Irish cream liqueur, or blended frozen with vanilla ice cream.

Simple servings

On the rocks

Enjoy the pure, rich flavour of Kahlúa simply over ice.

With milk or cream

A simple and delicious combination — pour Kahlúa into a glass with milk or cream for a smooth, coffee-flavoured treat.

In hot drinks

Add a splash of Kahlúa to hot coffee, hot chocolate or a warm cup of milk for a comforting drink with a kick.
